## Notes for implementers

1. **Lookahead.** The grammar is LL(2) at member level (see §4 comment) and needs one extra rule for `HandlerArg` (`Ident ':'`). Expressions are parsed by Pratt with binding powers from `spec/language.md` §6.1; after parsing a comparison (`<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=`) or equality operand pair, seeing another operator of the same level is `E1010`.
2. **`'>'` in types.** Types appear only after `:` or `->` and in `ArrayLength` contexts, so `<`/`>` there are never comparison operators. The parser therefore splits a `>>` or `>=` token in type context into the closing `>` and the rest (decision 0023).
3. **Trailing separators.** Commas may trail in parameter lists, argument lists, import lists, handler arguments and array literals. In descriptor literals the separator is `;` and a trailing `;` is optional. Struct fields, field initialisers, statements and declarations always end in `;`.
4. **Corpus.** Every production must be exercised by at least one positive fixture in `tests/syntax/pass/` and every `[S: …]` rule by at least one negative fixture in `tests/semantics/fail/` (or `tests/syntax/fail/` for the rules the lexer and parser enforce). `tools/grammar-coverage` (M2, decision 0042) measures which productions and top-level alternatives the parser actually builds from the positive fixtures, and checks that every code of every rule `[S: rule-id: …]` is reported by a fail fixture declaring `// covers: [S: rule-id]`; its test fails on anything uncovered, and the M2 gate requires nothing uncovered.
5. **Nested block comments** make the lexical grammar non-regular. Tree-sitter needs an external scanner for them (as the Rust grammar does); GBNF expresses them by recursion. Adapters that cannot support them must be documented as not accepting nested comments, and the corpus records that as a known adapter difference.

## Frozen subset (M2)

Frozen on **2026-10-04** at commit `a729b82` (branch `task/m2-11-grammar-coverage`, the commit at which every point below became covered; it reaches `main` with the merge of work item M2-11), decision 0042.

- **Syntax: the whole reference grammar.** The parser implements every production of `spec/grammar.ebnf` — 73 productions, 116 top-level alternatives — and `tools/grammar-coverage` measures each as built from at least one fixture of `tests/syntax/pass/` (`Reserved` from fail fixtures reporting `E0013` on each of its 30 words). There is no unimplemented syntax left to freeze out; the frozen subset of v0.1 is the grammar as written, read with decision 0023: `material` is accepted as a field name (item 1, a deliberate deviation from the `Keyword` production), `>>` and `>=` are split in type context (item 2), and the placement rules of `E1040` (item 5).
- **Restrictions: 11 rules `[S: …]`, all covered.** Seven are enforced by this build and each of their codes has a fail fixture declaring the rule: `reserved-identifiers` (E0011, E0012), `import-specifier` (E2030–E2034), `stage-name` (E4020, E4021, E4901), `param-only-in-prefab` (E1040), `scene-object-kind` (E5014), `array-type-arguments` (E3003, E3031), `simple-statement` (E1020, E3061). Four check constructs that parse but are still gated (`E9010`, decision 0025) and are covered by a fixture holding the violation, which reports the gate today: `prefab-no-nested-entity` and `prefab-instance-params` (prefabs, M5), `bind-bindable-field` (`bind`, M3), `lifecycle-signature` (lifecycle functions, M3).
- **Changing it.** A change to `spec/grammar.ebnf` after the freeze needs a decision record, fixtures for what it adds, and the counts pinned in `tools/grammar-coverage/tests/coverage.rs`. The work items that lift the four gates remove their entries from the tool's `GATED_RULES` and re-bless the `gate_*` fixtures that declare those rules.
