# 0220 — Blend spaces and layer fades

Status: accepted. Date: 2026-10-27. Extends [0196](0196-animation-blends-and-crowds.md)
and [0219](0219-complete-composable-animation.md). Implements acceptance items 2
and 3 of 0219. Runtime ABI 1 and extension API 1 are retained; there is no new
Mtek syntax (the language surface is decision 0223). The normative text is section
1 of [`spec/animation.md`](../animation.md).

## Decision

**2D blend spaces.** `player.blend` (and `AnimationController.setBlendSpace /
setBlendParameter`) plays 1 to 32 `{clip, x, y}` samples at a parameter `(x, y)`.
The weights are the barycentric coordinates in the Delaunay triangulation of the
sample points; outside the convex hull they are those of the nearest hull point;
collinear samples interpolate piecewise-linearly along their line, which makes
every 1D case the existing locomotion behaviour. Duplicate points are rejected
with a `RangeError`. Cocircular ties (every grid cell) are broken by a fixed
integer-hash lifting perturbation, so the layout alone determines the result.
Weights are nonnegative, sum to one, are exactly one at their sample, and are
continuous in the parameter; tests assert each, including along paths over edges,
vertices and the hull. All active clips share one normalized phase advancing at
the weighted mean of the inverse durations, so moving the parameter never jumps
the phase; markers, root motion, layers, overrides and IK work as for locomotion.

**One engine, two views.** The implementation is one class, `BlendSpace`; the
1D `player.locomotion` API of 0196 is a thin view of it (thresholds are `x`,
`y = 0`) with its validation, errors and numbers unchanged, and every locomotion
test passes unmodified. Mounted `animation.play/pause/resume/seek/time/...` and
the inspector controls therefore also drive any active 2D blend.

**Inspection.** `AnimationInspection.blend` lists samples with their current
weights, the parameter and the phase. `mode` stays `"locomotion"` for any active
blend space instead of adding `"blend"`: the value already means "phase-synchronized
blend replaces the base clip", the inspector package consumes it, and a new value
would force every consumer to change for no new information. The 1D
`locomotion` field is kept and is non-null only when all samples have `y = 0`.

**Layer fades.** `player.layers.fade(id, weight, seconds)` (and
`AnimationController.fadeLayer`) moves a layer's weight from its current value to
the target along a smoothstep, the curve clip transitions already use. It is
advanced by the player clock independently of the layer clock and so is
independent of the frame partition; a later `fade` is an interruption that starts
from the weight reached; `set` cancels the fade and applies its weight at once;
the layer's phase is untouched; zero seconds is immediate; a layer faded to zero
stays until removed. Inspection adds `targetWeight` and `fadeRemaining`.

## Why

Smooth 2D control (speed against turn rate, strafe directions) is the common case
that the 1D blend cannot express, and layer weights could only jump or be animated
by the host every frame. Delaunay barycentric weights are the standard that makes a
set of scattered clips behave like a continuous surface; a hull clamp and a
collinear fallback make the weights total functions, so no parameter can produce a
missing pose.

## Consequences and limits

- Weights are a function of the unscaled `(x, y)`. Nearest-hull projection and
  triangle shapes depend on the axis scales; hosts should scale axes to comparable
  ranges. Nearly collinear layouts give thin triangles with steep, but finite and
  continuous, slopes.
- Setting up a new layout tests every sample triple against every sample, at most
  `C(32,3) * 32`, a few milliseconds; identical point lists reuse the solver.
- The blend does not smooth the parameter or time-warp clips; clips must have
  aligned cycles, as in 0196.
- One more state to take care of: `locomotion.set` and `blend.set` replace each
  other, since they configure the same blend.
- `runtime.min.js` grows by about 4.3 kB (1.7 kB gzipped), measured against the
  base commit.
- The prebuilt runtime in `crates/mtek-cli/prebuilt` must be regenerated by the
  integrator (`node scripts/prebuilt-runtime.mjs --write`).
- `AnimationLayerInspection` has two new required fields and `AnimationInspection`
  one (`blend`); producers outside the runtime must add them.

Deviations from 0219: none.
